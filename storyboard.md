# RideGuard — 60-Second Pitch · Storyboard

5 speakers · 6 shots · ~150 words · film horizontally (landscape) on a phone at 1080p or 4K.
Record audio with the phone close to speakers (or a second phone as a mic) — audio quality matters more than video.

## Shot 1 — The Hook · 0:00–0:06 · Person 1

- **Framing:** Medium close-up (chest up), Person 1 centered, looking just past the lens
- **Camera:** Eye level, start slightly wide and slowly push in (or zoom in post)
- **Background:** Plain wall or bike rack — keep it clean
- **Action:** Steps toward camera on "Every year"
- **On-screen text (0:02):** `Every year, thousands of cyclists crash alone.`
- **Audio beat:** Start with a bike-bell ding or tire-skid sound effect under the line

> Line: "Every year, thousands of cyclists crash — and when no one's around, help can come too late."

## Shot 2 — The Problem · 0:06–0:15 · Person 2

- **Framing:** Wide shot of Person 2 standing next to a bike
- **Camera:** Eye level, locked off (tripod)
- **Cutaway b-roll (0:10):** 2–3 seconds — someone looking at a phone / a fallen bike on the ground
- **On-screen text (0:08):** `A fall. No phone. No help.`
- **Tip:** Person 2 can gesture at the bike on "ride alone"

> Line: "Cyclists often ride alone. A fall can leave someone unconscious or unable to reach their phone. That's the problem we're solving."

## Shot 3 — The Solution · 0:15–0:30 · Person 3 ⭐ hero shot

- **Framing 1 (0:15–0:21):** Medium, Person 3 holding the 3D model at chest height, model facing camera
- **Framing 2 (0:21–0:30):** INSERT — close-up of the device in hands, slow rotate
- **Camera:** Eye level on the speaker; high angle ~30° looking down on the device for the insert
- **On-screen text (0:18):** `RideGuard — fall detection + GPS + SIM`
- **Screen recording insert (0:26):** 2–3 s of the 3D viewer's **Exploded view** animation, showing PCB / SIM800L / GPS labels
- **Tip:** This is your showcase — rehearse holding the model steady and angled toward the lens

> Line: "Meet RideGuard — a lightweight device that clips onto your bike. It detects a fall with motion sensors, and instantly sends your GPS location as an SMS alert to an emergency number, through its built-in SIM module."

## Shot 4 — The Cancel Button · 0:30–0:40 · Person 4

- **Framing:** Close-up of the device, Person 4's thumb in frame
- **Camera:** High angle, slow top-down tilt; keep the red button dead-center at first
- **Action:** Thumb taps the cancel button right on "cancellation button" — count the tap
- **On-screen text (0:33):** `False alarm? Cancel in 15 seconds.`
- **Optional graphic:** A shrinking ring around the button, like a countdown timer

> Line: "Bumped the bike by accident? A cancellation button gives you 15 seconds to stop the alert — so help is only called when you truly need it."

## Shot 5 — The Impact · 0:40–0:50 · Person 5

- **Framing:** Medium two-shot — Person 5 with a cyclist mounting the bike behind (b-roll style)
- **Camera:** Eye level, slight lateral dolly/pan (or pan in post)
- **On-screen text (0:43):** `No phone. No app. Just ride.`
- **Audio beat:** Light upbeat music starts swelling here if you use a track

> Line: "No phone, no app, no extra charges — just wear it and ride. Faster rescue means fewer serious injuries."

## Shot 6 — The Close · 0:50–1:00 · All 5

- **Framing:** Wide group shot, all 5 in a slight arc, device held up front-center
- **Camera:** Eye level, maybe 5° low angle for a confident finish; hold still
- **Action:** All 5 point at the device on the final word, then freeze — cut to title card
- **On-screen text (0:55):** `RideGuard — because every second counts.` (title card, logo if you have one)
- **Audio beat:** Music button ends on the freeze

> Line (together): "Small device. Big difference. RideGuard — because every second counts."

## Production checklist

- [ ] Charge phone, clear 2–3 GB, film landscape 1080p/30fps minimum
- [ ] Lock exposure/focus (tap-hold on iPhone / pro mode on Android)
- [ ] Record 2 takes per shot; clap once at the start of each take for syncing
- [ ] Grab b-roll: fallen bike, phone screen, hands, wheels spinning (5–10 s each)
- [ ] Screen-record the 3D viewer explode animation (10 s, the included `rideguard-viewer.html`)
- [ ] Edit order: shots 1→6, lay text in at the timestamps above, music under from shot 2
- [ ] Export 1080p, check final length ≤ 60 s before submitting
